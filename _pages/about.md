## About Me

I am an **FPGA Engineer** at **Whirlybird Electronics** in Vasai, specializing in **Digital Design** and **FPGA/ASIC architectures**. With over 2 years of professional experience, my expertise lies in bridging the gap between high-level software automation and low-level hardware description.

Before moving to Vasai, I worked as a Digital Design Engineer at **RFMW Innovations Lab** in Bengaluru, where I focused on RTL development and RF testing. I am passionate about **Verilog** and **Python scripting**, particularly in creating tools that automate hardware design.

### Technical Expertise:
* **Embedded & Digital Design:** FPGA/ASIC Design, RTL development, Embedded Systems.
* **Languages:** Verilog, VHDL, C/C++, Python.
* **Automation:** Python-based hardware code generation and data analysis.
* **Past Experience:** RF & Microwave technologies, system-level testing.

### Beyond the Lab
