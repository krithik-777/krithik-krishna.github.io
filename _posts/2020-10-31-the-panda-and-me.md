They say the best things happen when you aren't looking. For me, that day was **October 31, 2020**. 

While the world was navigating the chaos of COVID, we were navigating the initial stages of fun texts and campus chats. When we finally met in person at the campus cafes, it was clear we were a study in contrasts. She is incredibly talkative—full of stories and energy—and I have always been the quieter guy, the one who finds peace in listening to her.

We had mutually opposite interests, but as time went on, I realized I wasn't able to move on alone. There was no grand, cinematic proposal; instead, it was a quiet, mutual realization. We both just knew. 

Of course, like any journey, it hasn't always been perfectly smooth. Sometimes we fight, but at the end of the day, it all comes back to a peaceful end. It just proves that theory: **opposites always attract.** Most importantly, my Panda has been there with me through all the ups and downs. She helped me immensely to become the person I am today and to reach where I am currently in my career and life. We decided together that we wanted this to last \"till death.\" Looking back, she is truly the best thing that has ever happened in my life. ❤️🐼
