My first trip to Vasai started on the night of **March 7th**. It wasn't just a move for a new job; it was my first time experiencing a **1AC train journey**. 

The highlight of the trip wasn't just the comfort, but the company. I met a guy traveling from Bengaluru to Gujarat with **two beautiful Persian cats**. Spending a full day journeying with them made the transition from the tech-hub of Bengaluru to the industrial landscape of Vasai feel much more like an adventure than a chore.

There’s something peaceful about watching the tracks fly by when you have two furry "cuties" keeping you company in the coupe!
